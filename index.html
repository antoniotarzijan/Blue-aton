How a project works

From the first sale to the renewed contract.

Three stages, one accountable partner. Open each one to see exactly what we do.

STAGE 1

Business Development

Finding and closing your first customers in Latin America.
  • Dedicated B2B sales development connecting your solution directly with mining operators through customer discovery and market segmentation.
  • A tailored go-to-market plan built around how Latin American mining companies actually buy technology.
  • Selection and oversight of distributors, integrators, and agents to expand your reach beyond what a direct team can cover.
STAGE 2

Project Management

Getting the pilot into the field and proving it works.
  • Design, execution, and metric validation of proof-of-concept (POC) and pilot projects on site.
  • Deployment logistics for remote and high-altitude operations, where compliance and local knowledge determine whether a pilot ships on time.
  • Every site and every customer is different — we plan around that, not a template.
STAGE 3

Customer Success

Keeping the technology adopted, renewed, and expanding.
  • Tier 1 and Tier 2 support with Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking staff, in the same time zone as the site.
  • Operational change management: we train operators, engineers, and superintendents to drive real adoption, not just installation.
  • Ongoing customer success management that drives renewals, contract upgrades, and expansion across sites.
